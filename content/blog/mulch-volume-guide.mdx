## Why Accurate Yardage Matters

Ordering too little mulch creates uneven bed coverage and extra delivery delays. Ordering too much can leave material exposed to weather before use.

## Measure Beds In Three Steps

1. Break the area into rectangles.
2. Measure each section in feet.
3. Add sections together for total square footage.

## Convert Depth To Cubic Yards

Use this formula:

`(length x width x depth in feet) / 27 = cubic yards`

If your depth is in inches, divide inches by `12` first.

## Select The Right Product

- Use dyed mulch for long color hold.
- Use hemlock mulch for a natural premium finish.

For mixed properties, place bulk order first and schedule bagged touch-up material as pickup.

## Final Check Before Checkout

- Confirm delivery access constraints.
- Confirm bed depth target (2 to 3 inches).
- Save your calculator output before adding to cart.
