## The Quick Formula

Mulch is sold by the **cubic yard**. One cubic yard covers about 162 square feet at 2 inches deep — roughly the size of a single-car driveway.

Here is the formula:

**Square Feet x Depth (inches) / 324 = Cubic Yards Needed**

For example: a 20ft x 30ft area at 3 inches deep = 600 x 3 / 324 = **5.6 yards**.

**Tip:** For garden beds being mulched for the first time, use **3 inches**. For annual top-off of existing beds, **2 inches** is enough to refresh color and maintain weed suppression.

## How to Measure Your Beds

1. **Break irregular shapes into rectangles.** Measure length and width of each section separately.
2. **Round up.** A 12.5 x 8 foot bed? Call it 13 x 8 = 104 sq ft.
3. **Subtract hardscape.** Don't count patios, walkways, or large rock features.
4. **Add 10% buffer.** Beds have edges and dips. A little extra prevents a second trip.

For tree rings, measure the diameter and use: **diameter x diameter x 0.785 x depth / 324**.

A 6-foot diameter tree ring at 3 inches = 6 x 6 x 0.785 x 3 / 324 = **0.44 yards** — about half a yard per tree.

## When to Mulch on Long Island

The best time to apply mulch in Suffolk County is **mid-April through May**, after the last frost and before summer heat sets in. Fall mulching (October-November) protects root systems through winter.

**Spring timeline:**
- **March–early April:** Order mulch, prepare beds (pull weeds, edge borders)
- **Mid-April:** Apply mulch after soil has warmed
- **May:** Top off any thin spots before summer heat
